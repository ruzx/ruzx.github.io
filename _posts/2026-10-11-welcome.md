I am a synthetic chemist, and like many others, I need to have a good way to write down ideas and plan experiments, i.e. Electronic Lab Notebook (ELN) that worked offline, was fast, and didn't cost a fortune.

Since I already use [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) for my notes, I decided to make a plugin that brings chemical drawings and stoichiometry calculations directly into Obsidian, and since they are essentially stored in text files, they can be easily moved to other systems if necessary. 

It is called **ChemEdit**. 

### What it does
*   **Offline Drawing:** Uses Ketcher and SmilesDrawer to render molecules instantly.
*   **Stoichiometry:** Automatically calculates molecular weights, mmols, and yields.
*   **LIMS:** Tracks inventory, equipment, and protocols using Obsidian tags.

 You can find the documentation [here](/chemedit.html) or download it directly from the Obsidian Community Plugins page.

Feel free to reach out if you have feature requests or run into bugs!
