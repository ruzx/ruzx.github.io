About

Ph.D. chemist with more than ten years of research in sustainable catalysis, electro-organic synthesis and reaction optimisation. I develop C–H functionalisation and late-stage functionalisation methods for complex molecules, map catalytic cycles to make reactions robust, and take lab-scale cascades towards scalable, greener processes. I also build small open software tools for chemists.

  • Electro-organic synthesis
  • C–H functionalisation
  • Late-stage functionalisation
  • Isotope labelling
  • Process scale-up

Apps for chemists

They run in the browser and install as offline apps on Windows, macOS, Android and iOS (use the browser's Install or Add to Home Screen).

ChemEdit
Structure editor & electronic lab notebook

Draw structures and double-click to edit them again. Files are saved straight to a folder on your computer (SVG, Molfile, SDF, CDXML…). Keep experiments with stoichiometry, reaction schemes and a lab book. Works offline; nothing leaves your device.

Open in browser →
Molecule Arcade
Puzzle games for organic chemistry

Four short games for students and teachers: link transformations, forge multistep routes, draw mystery molecules from clues, and play functional-group bingo. Works offline.
