About

Ph.D. chemist with more than ten years of research in sustainable catalysis, electro-organic synthesis and reaction optimisation. I develop C–H functionalisation and late-stage functionalisation methods for complex molecules, map catalytic cycles to make reactions robust, and take lab-scale cascades towards scalable, greener processes. I also build small free software tools for teaching and learning chemistry.

Research

I develop redox methods that make synthesis cleaner and more selective. By combining catalysis, electrochemistry and electroanalysis, I aim for reactions that functionalise C–H bonds, modify drug-like molecules at a late stage and install isotope labels — and that are understood well enough mechanistically to be scaled and used by others.

Educational apps

Free tools made mainly for teaching and learning chemistry — for students, teachers and anyone curious. They run in the browser and install as offline apps on Windows, macOS, Android and iOS (use the browser's Install or Add to Home Screen).

ChemEdit
Structure editor & lab notebook for teaching

A free educational tool for students and teachers: draw structures and double-click to edit them again, save them as ordinary files (SVG, Molfile, SDF, CDXML…), and practise keeping a lab notebook with stoichiometry, reaction schemes and a lab book. Works offline; nothing leaves your device.

ChemGame
Organic chemistry learning games

Four short learning games for organic chemistry courses: link transformations, put synthetic routes in order, draw mystery molecules from clues, and play functional-group bingo. Free, offline, no sign-up.
