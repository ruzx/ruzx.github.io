This policy covers the web apps **ChemEdit** (`/chemedit/`) and **ChemGame** (`/chemgame/`) on ruzx.github.io. It also covers their versions in app stores (Google Play, Microsoft Store), which show exactly the same web apps.

## In short

The apps collect **no personal data**. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Everything you create stays on your own device.

## What is stored, and where

- **Your files** (structures, lab-notebook notes and photos) are saved only on your device, in a folder you choose or in the browser's own storage. They are never uploaded to any server.
- **Settings and game scores** are kept in your browser's local storage on your device.
- **Offline copy of the app:** the browser stores the app's own files so it works without internet.

You can delete all of this at any time: remove the files from your folder, or clear the site's data in your browser or app settings.

## Data that leaves your device

- **Optional name lookup (ChemEdit):** when you type a chemical *name* and you are online, that text is sent to the [OPSIN service](https://opsin.ch.cam.ac.uk/) (University of Cambridge) and/or [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) (US National Library of Medicine) to get the structure. Nothing else is sent. You can switch this off in ChemEdit → Settings.
- **Hosting:** the site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes; see the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Camera and files

ChemEdit only uses the camera or file picker when **you** tap "Photo", "Import" or "Choose a folder". Photos are saved to your own folder or browser storage.

## Children

The apps are made for education and are suitable for all ages. No personal data is collected from anyone.
