Profile

Ph.D. chemist with more than ten years of research in sustainable catalysis, electro-organic synthesis and reaction optimisation. I develop C–H functionalisation and late-stage functionalisation methods for complex molecules, map catalytic cycles to make reactions robust, and take lab-scale cascades towards scalable, greener processes. I also build small free software tools for teaching and learning chemistry.

Core competencies

Chemistry
Organic electrosynthesis & electrocatalysis (anodic/cathodic, paired), sustainable/green chemistry, photocatalysis, metal catalysis, cross-coupling (XEC, HAT), C–H activation, late-stage functionalisation, catalytic cascades, redox chemistry, isotope labelling (D, ¹³CD₃), click and supramolecular chemistry, mechanistic studies
Process & scale-up
Reaction scale-up to preparative scale, high-throughput screening and Bayesian optimisation, flow and electro reactors, process miniaturisation, data-driven optimisation workflows
Analytical
NMR (1D/2D), MS (GC/LC-MS, ESI, HRMS, MALDI-TOF), GC, HPLC, UV-Vis, fluorescence, IR, cyclic voltammetry, potentiometry, synchrotron X-ray techniques, XRD, DLS, rheometry, thermal analysis
Technical
Glovebox and Schlenk techniques; microwave, photo-, electro-, flow and high-pressure reactors; crystal growth; chromatography systems; instrument maintenance and troubleshooting
Software
ChemDraw, SciFinder/Reaxys, MestReNova/TopSpin, Mercury, Origin, LaTeX, Zotero/EndNote, automation; scientific illustration (Inkscape, Blender)
