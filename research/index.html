Research

I develop redox methods that make synthesis cleaner and more selective. By combining catalysis, electrochemistry and electroanalysis, I aim for reactions that functionalise C–H bonds, modify drug-like molecules at a late stage and install isotope labels — and that are understood well enough mechanistically to be scaled and used by others.

Research themes

Electrosynthesis with catalysis

current · KTH

Method development that pairs molecular catalysts with electrodes, using electroanalysis (CV, controlled-potential and constant-current electrolysis) to understand mechanisms and design better catalysts and conditions.

  • Molecular electrocatalysis
  • Cyclic voltammetry
  • Constant-current / potential electrolysis
  • HTE & data-driven optimisation

Metalla-electrocatalytic C–H functionalisation

2023–2025 · Uppsala

Earth-abundant metal catalysts (e.g. cobalt) driven by electricity instead of chemical oxidants, for C–H functionalisation and late-stage modification of bioactive molecules at room temperature.

  • Co/Ni electrocatalysis
  • Late-stage functionalisation
  • Scale-up

Electrochemical isotope labelling

2018–2021 · KAUST

Nickel-catalysed electrochemical cross-electrophile coupling to install CD₃ and ¹³CD₃ groups into drug-like molecules — simple, inexpensive routes to labelled compounds for DMPK studies.

  • Ni-catalysed XEC
  • Deuteration
  • ¹³C/D labelling
  1. Sitdikov R. R., Steverlynck J., Nikolaenko P., Kale A., Rueping M. “Trideuteromethylation of alkyl and aryl bromides by nickel-catalyzed electrochemical reductive cross-electrophile coupling”. Synlett, 2024. doi:10.1055/s-0042-1751558

Electroanalysis, redox mediators and sensors

2011–2023 · KTH, Kazan

Redox-active probes and mediators (ferrocene, Ru/Os complexes, phenazine and Neutral Red macrocycles) for ion-to-electron transduction, voltammetric sensing and aptasensors.

  • Voltammetry
  • Redox mediators
  • Modified electrodes

(Thia)calixarene and pillararene receptors, glycodendrimers against multiresistant bacteria, and mannose-derived organogelators — the foundation in synthesis, recognition and materials that I build on.

Educational software

ChemEdit
Structure editor & lab notebook for teaching

A free educational tool for students and teachers: draw structures and double-click to edit them again, save them as ordinary files (SVG, Molfile, SDF, CDXML…), and practise keeping a lab notebook with stoichiometry, reaction schemes and a lab book. Works offline; nothing leaves your device.
