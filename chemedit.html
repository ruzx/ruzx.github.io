Introduction

ChemEdit is a powerful Electronic Lab Notebook (ELN) and chemical drawing suite built directly into Obsidian. It allows synthetic chemists to draw molecules, calculate stoichiometry, track inventory, and map synthesis pathways completely offline.

Installation

Currently, ChemEdit is installed manually or via BRAT while it awaits approval into the official Obsidian community store.

  1. Download the latest release from GitHub.
  2. Extract the folder into your vault's .obsidian/plugins/ directory.
  3. Restart Obsidian and enable ChemEdit in Community Plugins.

Drawing Molecules

ChemEdit uses an embedded version of Ketcher, meaning you can draw complex chemical structures natively inside Obsidian without relying on external websites.

  • New Drawing: Click the Hexagon icon in the left ribbon to open the canvas.
  • Supported Formats: ChemEdit supports saving as SMILES, .mol, .cdxml, and .ket.
  • Editing: Simply double-click any rendered chemical structure in your note to re-open the editor.

Inline Structures

You can render molecules directly inside sentences or Markdown tables. This is perfect for writing reaction procedures.

Simply use the prefix $smiles= followed by your SMILES string.

The reaction was quenched to yield $smiles=CC(=O)OC1=CC=CC=C1C(=O)O as a white solid.

The ELN Block

The core of ChemEdit is the interactive ELN block. You can insert a new experiment by opening the Command Palette (Ctrl+P) and selecting ChemEdit: Open ELN Dashboard, then clicking + New Experiment.

This creates a special YAML code block that looks like this:

```eln
status: Planned
reactants:
  - smiles: "CC1=CC(=O)CCC1"
    eq: 1
    mass: 150
products:
  - smiles: "CC1=CC(O)CCC1"
```

Obsidian will automatically render this block as a beautiful, interactive reaction scheme and stoichiometry table.

Stoichiometry Engine

When using the ELN block, ChemEdit acts as a live calculator:

  • Auto-Molecular Weights: It calculates MWs instantly and offline.
  • Live Math: Double-click the table cells in Obsidian to type a mass. The mmols and product theoretical yields calculate instantly.
  • Yields: Enter your isolated mass in the Products table to calculate your percentage yield. It turns green if >80%, and red if <30%.

Inventory, Orders & Dashboards

ChemEdit turns Obsidian into a full LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System).

By adding specific tags to your notes (e.g., tags: [inventory] or tags: [order]), the plugin pulls them into a central database. Open the Dashboards from the Command Palette to see sortable, filterable tables of your entire lab's stock!

Synthesis Pathways

ChemEdit can read the reactants and products across your entire vault and automatically stitch them together into an interactive mind-map.

Use the code block ```synthesis-map``` in a note to generate a flowchart of your current project's progress.

Web Sharing

Need to show a reaction to a collaborator who doesn't use Obsidian? Click the 🔗 Share button on any ELN block. It will generate a web link that you can send on Slack or Email. They can view the reaction and even edit the stoichiometry right in their browser!
